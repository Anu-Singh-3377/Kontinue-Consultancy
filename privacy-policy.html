Privacy Policy

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and with respect for privacy.

Kontinue Consultancy processes personal data only where necessary for providing professional services, responding to enquiries, or managing client communications. We aim to collect only the information needed to deliver our services effectively.

Information may include contact details, basic business information, and any documentation required for professional support. We keep such information confidential and store it securely in accordance with applicable legal and professional standards.

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared only with trusted service providers or professional advisers where required for the delivery of services or compliance obligations.

Clients may request information about the personal data we hold, and they may also ask us to correct or update details where appropriate. Please contact our office if you have any privacy-related questions.
